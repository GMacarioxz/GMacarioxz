## 🌷 About Me

My journey into technology started when I was **16 years old**, when I decided to study **Systems Development at SENAI**.

At first, entering tech felt challenging, especially because I didn’t see many women around me in that environment. Even so, curiosity pushed me forward — and what began as an interest gradually became a real passion for building solutions, solving problems, and understanding how technology can create impact.

Over time, I developed a strong interest in **Backend Development**, **Data**, and **Cloud Computing**. I enjoy designing APIs, working with databases, understanding business rules, and connecting different technologies to turn ideas into real applications.

One of the projects I’m most proud of is **Fatal Lady**, a full-stack e-commerce platform where I worked mainly on the backend using **Python**, **FastAPI**, **PostgreSQL**, authentication flows, integrations, and cloud deployment.

Today, I’m studying **Systems Analysis and Development at FIAP**, expanding my knowledge in **Java, Python, C#, databases, cloud computing, and artificial intelligence**.

Beyond technical growth, I also carry a personal goal: in the future, I would love to **become a teacher** and help inspire more women to pursue careers in technology 💜
