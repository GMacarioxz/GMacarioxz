## About Me

Hi! I'm Gabrielly Macario, a Software Developer and Systems Analysis and Development student at FIAP, based in São Paulo, Brazil.

My journey into technology started when I was 16, when I decided to study Systems Development at SENAI. At the time, I was entering a field where I didn't see many women around me, but my curiosity and determination encouraged me to keep exploring.

What started as an interest in understanding how technology works gradually became a passion for building solutions, solving problems, and transforming ideas into real applications.

Throughout my technical education, I had the opportunity to work on projects that challenged me to go beyond the classroom. One of the most meaningful was Fatal Lady, a full-stack e-commerce platform where I worked extensively with Python, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, authentication, payment flows, integrations, and cloud deployment.

This experience strengthened my interest in backend development and showed me how much I enjoy designing systems, understanding business rules, and connecting different technologies to create functional solutions.

Along the way, I also gained professional experience in IT support and software development, which helped me develop technical skills, adaptability, and a broader understanding of technology in real-world environments.

Today, I'm pursuing my degree at FIAP and expanding my knowledge in Java, Python, C#, databases, cloud computing, and artificial intelligence.

I'm particularly interested in Backend Development, Data Engineering, and Cloud Computing. I believe data becomes truly valuable when it helps people make better decisions, and I enjoy exploring how software and cloud technologies can turn information into meaningful solutions.

Beyond my technical goals, I hope to contribute to a more inclusive technology industry. In the future, I would love to become a teacher, share my knowledge, and inspire more women to pursue careers in technology.

For me, technology is not just about writing code. It's about continuous learning, solving meaningful problems, and creating opportunities for others.

## Featured Projects

### Fatal Lady — E-commerce Platform

A full-stack e-commerce application developed as part of my technical education at SENAI.

I worked mainly on backend development, implementing authentication, database integration, order management, payment flows, and cloud deployment.

**Main features:**
- User authentication and Google OAuth.
- Product catalog, shopping cart, and checkout.
- Order and payment management.
- Email automation and account recovery.
- Administrative dashboard.
- Cloud deployment with HTTPS.

**Technologies:** Python, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, SQLAlchemy, Jinja2, Supabase, and Microsoft Azure.

---

### AssetHub — IT Asset Management

An application developed to manage IT assets, equipment, users, locations, and asset movements.

The project focuses on organizing asset information and tracking equipment throughout its lifecycle.

**Technologies:** Python, FastAPI, SQLite, and SQLAlchemy.

---

### Turma do Bem — FIAP Challenge

An academic project developed in partnership with Turma do Bem, a Brazilian nonprofit organization dedicated to promoting social inclusion through oral healthcare.

The proposed solution integrates software development, data management, and artificial intelligence to support fundraising, donor management, and institutional relationship management.

**Technologies:** Java, Python, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, relational databases, and AI.

[View Project](https://github.com/GMacarioxz/turma-do-bem)
